Cola Wars

Game Info

  • Genre: strategy
  • Number of Players: 1-4
  • Age Range: 14+
  • Game Duration: 90-120 minutes
  • AI Usage: Yes

Description

Cola wars os a strategy board game that was inspired byu the real-life cola wars from the 1980s. The goal of the game is to have the most market share tokens by the end of the game wins!

It is played on two battlefields, the head and shelf space. Head space prioritizes brand awareness, while shelf space focuses on making your product easily accessible. At the end of the in game year, dice are rolled to determine the consumer's thoughts about the products, annd whoever is in the right Head and Shelf space wins the market.

The game also has a feature where players can choose to hire executives, allowing them to run advertisment campaigns and other promotions. With this aspect, budgeting will play a large role in the game.

Reviews

Cola Wars, despite the fascinating concept, has not recvieved positive feedback from the community. One such review, from user DougieBarx, expresses their concern stating,

"Buyer Beware: Sketchy publisher marketing with AI simulations. Hopefully this gets a reprint from a legit publisher in the future because the design looks fun, shame about the production."
